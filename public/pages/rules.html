Gameplay rules

Rules

These are the core runner rules used by the current V24.5 client.

Play RunnerNFT Gallery
  • Lives: every run begins with three lives.
  • Single hit: losing one life does not reset score, time, stage, obstacles or the active run.
  • Post-hit protection: a short protection window prevents one collision from consuming multiple lives.
  • Third life: when all three lives are used, gameplay stops and a three-minute retry lock begins.
  • Retry: Start, Space, tap and jump inputs cannot start a run while the retry lock is active.
  • Checkpoint: after the lock expires, wallet-connected verified progression resumes from the highest sequential milestone recorded as minted by the protocol.
  • Minting: milestones are sequential and a verified on-chain run is required for mint eligibility.
